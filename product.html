CAMPUSBRIDGE PRODUCT

Tools built around the student journey.

CampusBridge brings opportunity discovery, verification and application tracking into one student-focused platform.

01

Opportunity Discovery

Browse scholarships, internships, competitions, training programmes and other education and career opportunities.

02

Source Verification

Check opportunity sources and receive verification information designed to help students identify suspicious or potentially risky links.

03

Application Tracking

Students can view submitted applications and follow their progress through available application statuses.

04

Student Account

A dedicated account experience allows students to access their profile and application information securely.

05

Organization Opportunities

Organizations can publish opportunities and reach students through a focused opportunity platform.

06

Opportunity Intelligence

Structured opportunity information helps students understand what an opportunity is, who it is for, where it comes from and when it closes.
