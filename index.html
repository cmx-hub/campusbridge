TRUST & SAFETY

Don't just find an opportunity. Check it first.

CampusBridge uses a verification-focused approach to help students make safer decisions before they apply.

✓ Source check: identify the organization or original opportunity source.

✓ Link check: review suspicious or misleading application links.

✓ Information check: compare important details such as eligibility, deadlines and application instructions.

✓ Safety warning: recognize requests for sensitive information, payments or other suspicious activity.

Important: CampusBridge helps students assess opportunity risks, but students should always confirm critical details through the official organization or institution.
