Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

CampusBridge helps students discover scholarships, internships, training, competitions and other opportunities. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, password and profile information such as your institution, field of study, academic level, skills and interests.

Information you provide when contacting us may also be used to respond to your enquiry. Technical information may be processed when needed to operate, maintain or protect the platform.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, support relevant opportunity discovery, maintain platform security, respond to requests and improve CampusBridge.

3. Account Security

We take account security seriously. Keep your login credentials private and notify us if you suspect unauthorised access. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing and External Websites

We do not need your account password to help you contact us. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the platform. We aim to limit such use to relevant purposes.

CampusBridge may link to websites operated by universities, employers, organisations or other third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

5. Retention and Your Choices

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably needed for platform purposes, legitimate operational needs or applicable obligations. To ask about your account information or request its correction or deletion, contact us using the address below. We will review requests and explain any applicable limitations.

6. Children and Young Users

CampusBridge is designed for students seeking educational and career opportunities. Users should provide information they are authorised to share and seek appropriate guidance where required.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as CampusBridge develops. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact campusbridge.cm@gmail.com.
