HOW CAMPUSBRIDGE WORKS

Discover. Verify. Apply.

CampusBridge helps students move from finding an opportunity to making a more informed application decision.

01

Discover

Find scholarships, internships, competitions, training programmes and other education or career opportunities in one place.

02

Verify

Review the opportunity source and verification information before deciding whether an application is safe and relevant.

03

Apply

Follow the relevant source or application process and submit your application with greater confidence.

04

Track

Signed-in students can keep track of submitted applications and monitor their application status.
