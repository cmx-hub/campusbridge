TRUST & SAFETY

Verify before you apply.

CampusBridge is designed to help students make more informed decisions about online opportunities by making source and verification information easier to understand.

01

Source Verification

Opportunity sources can be checked before students follow a link or submit personal information.

02

Risk Awareness

Verification results are intended to help students understand potential warning signs rather than treating every opportunity as automatically safe.

03

Clear Information

Students can review opportunity details, source information and other available verification signals before applying.

04

Student Responsibility

CampusBridge supports safer decision-making, but students should still review official sources and avoid sharing sensitive information with untrusted websites.

05

Report Suspicious Sources

Suspicious opportunity sources can be reported so that potential issues can be reviewed and used to improve platform trust.

06

Security by Design

CampusBridge treats verification and responsible handling of student information as important parts of the platform experience.
