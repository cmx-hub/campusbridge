Our Business Model

Better access for students. Sustainable value for partners.

CampusBridge helps students discover opportunities and assess source credibility. Revenue is designed to come primarily from organizations and institutional partners, with optional premium services for students introduced after their value is validated.

Students

Free core access to discover opportunities, view details, and access basic source-risk information.

Optional premium services

The proposed model gives five free uses of an eligible premium feature before asking students to pay for additional use. The exact features and price will be validated with students.

Organizations

Organizations can submit opportunities for review and pay for publishing services or optional extra visibility.

Proposed starting prices

5,000 FCFA per standard listing; 10,000 FCFA for a featured listing; or 25,000 FCFA per month for a proposed subscription.

Institutions

Universities and educational institutions may partner with CampusBridge to improve opportunity discovery and access for their students.

Partnership contracts

Pricing will be negotiated according to the institution's needs, services, and scale.

How CampusBridge compares

Students already use opportunity aggregators, WhatsApp and Facebook groups, Telegram channels, LinkedIn, university notice boards, and ministry websites. These alternatives offer reach and convenience. CampusBridge aims to complement them with a more organized discovery experience and clearer source and review information.

Social groups

Fast sharing and community reach; posts can be difficult to search, compare, and revisit.

Aggregators

Broad opportunity coverage; CampusBridge's hypothesis is that Cameroon-focused discovery and transparent review states can serve local needs.

LinkedIn and official portals

Professional networking and authoritative announcements; CampusBridge aims to make opportunities from multiple sources easier to discover in one place.

Trust is part of the business model

Because fraudulent opportunities are a risk, payment must never guarantee approval or verification. CampusBridge's intended process is to collect organization details and supporting evidence, review the organization's identity and representative, check each opportunity's source and details, and publish only after the required review.

An HTTPS connection or valid domain alone does not prove that an organization is legitimate. Organization verification, source checks, and opportunity approval are separate decisions.

Our validation priorities

The initial survey of 23 respondents helps indicate that the problem exists among those surveyed; it does not establish total market size or willingness to pay. Our next milestones are to measure genuine registrations and repeat usage, track opportunity views and application-link clicks, interview potential paying organizations, and seek institutional pilot partners. We will report results only when supported by actual evidence.
