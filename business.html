CAMPUSBRIDGE BUSINESS MODEL

Connecting opportunity with the right students.

CampusBridge is designed as a multi-sided opportunity platform connecting students, organizations and institutions through a trusted digital experience.

01

Students

Students get easier access to relevant scholarships, internships, competitions, training and career opportunities.

02

Organizations

Organizations can use CampusBridge to publish opportunities and reach students who are looking for relevant programmes and opportunities.

03

Institutions

Institutions can benefit from a structured channel for communicating legitimate opportunities to students.

04

Free Student Access

Core opportunity discovery and access are designed to remain accessible to students.

05

Organization Services

Future organization-focused services may include enhanced opportunity publishing, visibility and campaign tools.

06

Premium Features

Optional premium services may provide additional value to students and organizations while keeping the core platform useful and accessible.
